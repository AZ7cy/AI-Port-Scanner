# Code of Conduct

This project has adopted the [Contributor Covenant, version 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. It applies to everyone who takes part in the project, in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project space.

## Summary

The full text linked above is the code of conduct. In short:

- Be respectful and considerate, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Do not harass, insult, or demean anyone.
- Do not share other people's private information, such as their IP addresses, hostnames, or scan results, without their permission.
- Do not use project spaces to plan, encourage, or share attacks on systems that you are not authorized to test.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to the maintainer, [@AZ7cy](https://github.com/AZ7cy), with GitHub's **Report content** option. You can find it in the **...** menu of the issue, pull request, or comment. Reports are handled privately.

To report a security vulnerability, follow the [Security Policy](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews every report and decides on a response, following the enforcement guidance in the Contributor Covenant. Responses range from a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban from the project.
